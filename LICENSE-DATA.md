The published files (`v1/meta.json`, `v1/cells.json`, `v1/tiles/*.json`) take
their licence from the upstream source of the run. Check `dataLicence` and
`credit` in `v1/meta.json` for the run you use.

## Copernicus Marine Service (`"dataLicence": "Copernicus-Marine-Service-Licence"`)

These files are a value-added product of Copernicus Marine Service products and
are provided **under the Copernicus Marine Service licence**, not CC BY:
https://marine.copernicus.eu/user-corner/service-commitments-and-licence

That licence is free and allows use for any purpose, including commercial, but
you must:

1. **Credit** Copernicus Marine clearly where the data is shown or accessed:
   > Generated using E.U. Copernicus Marine Service Information; https://doi.org/10.48670/moi-00017

   and please also credit "nz-coastal-waves".
2. **Keep records** documenting and tracing your use of the data, available to
   Copernicus Marine on reasonable notice (licence clause 2.6), and pass this
   requirement on to anyone you redistribute the data to.

Copernicus Marine has stated that its products are free of charge until the end
of the current service period (planned 30 June 2028).
